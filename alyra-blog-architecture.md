# Alyra Blog Architecture — SEO + GEO Content System

## 0. Strategic rationale (why this shape)

Money and growth point to one place: **B2B SaaS & Tech**, with **FinTech/Financial Services** as a tightly adjacent secondary vertical.

- SaaS/Tech and IT carry the highest AI-referral traffic of any industry tracked (2.80%, vs. under 1% for most verticals) — meaning AI search is already a live channel there, not a bet.
- SaaS buyers already budget for and buy new marketing "channels" readily (product-led growth culture, existing content teams) — the easiest vertical to actually close as clients.
- FinTech/Finance carries some of the highest SEO and link-building budgets of any vertical (finance takes 18.4% of high-budget link building spend, only beaten by iGaming) and shares the same digital-native, content-literate buyer as SaaS.
- E-commerce is a large market too, but it's a different buyer motion (retail/DTC marketing vs. B2B marketing) — treat as Tier 2 expansion once the core vertical has topical authority, not a launch pillar.
- Legal, Healthcare, iGaming: high spend but wrong buyer profile (compliance-heavy, slow sales cycles, or reputationally off-brand) — excluded from the initial architecture.

**Language split — EN + FR, same vertical, not a translation exercise:**
- **EN** = the global SaaS/FinTech buyer. The US alone accounts for over 40% of global GEO market revenue and is the most mature buyer market — this is where the volume and the competitive proof points are.
- **FR** = France specifically, timed to a real trigger event: Google AI Overviews and AI Mode only launched in France on July 22, 2026. French search interest in "référencement GEO" grew 17x on Google Trends between March 2025 and March 2026, and the share of French companies with zero GEO initiative is projected to fall from 27% to 9% within 2026. Authoritative French-language content on this topic is still thin — this is a short window of low competition against exploding demand.

Both languages target the *same* SaaS/FinTech ICP — this is a market-timing split, not an audience split.

## 1. Pillar structure

### Pillar 1 — GEO Category Education (TOFU)
Captures the exploding "what is this" search volume as the category itself goes mainstream. Broadest reach, lowest buyer intent, top of the funnel.

**Key terms:** generative engine optimization, GEO marketing, AEO (answer engine optimization), AI search optimization, AI Overviews, is SEO dead 2026, how ChatGPT picks sources, zero-click search

**Key use cases / article angles:**
- What is GEO and how is it different from SEO (definitional anchor page — the hub for this pillar)
- How ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews actually select and cite sources
- Why ranking #1 on Google no longer guarantees AI visibility (CTR/AI Overview data)
- Is SEO dead in 2026? What actually changed
- GEO glossary (AEO, AIO, LLMO, ASO — one page, built for AI citation itself)

**FR mirror:** same pillar, French-market hook — "Le lancement des AI Overviews en France : ce qui change pour votre entreprise" as the anchor/trigger post.

---

### Pillar 2 — Vertical Playbooks (MOFU)
Where buyer intent starts. This is the pillar that proves "we understand your business," not just GEO in the abstract.

**2a. GEO for B2B SaaS**
Key terms: GEO for SaaS, AEO for software companies, AI visibility for SaaS, best [category] software AI recommendation, product-led growth AI search
Key use cases:
- How B2B software buyers use ChatGPT during vendor evaluation (grounded in the finding that 90% of B2B buyers now use generative AI at some point in their buying journey)
- Getting cited in "best [category] tool" AI answers — the new G2/Capterra
- GEO for product-led growth companies (self-serve funnels depend on being *found*, not just ranked)
- Structuring a SaaS comparison page for AI extractability

**2b. GEO for FinTech / Financial Services**
Key terms: GEO for fintech, AI search fintech marketing, E-E-A-T for financial content, AI visibility banking
Key use cases:
- Why financial content needs materially higher authority signals to be trusted (and cited) by AI models
- GEO for fintech startups vs. established financial institutions
- Compliance-safe content structuring for AI citation (regulated-industry angle)

**FR mirror:** "GEO pour la French Tech" / "GEO pour les fintechs françaises" — anchor to the French SaaS/fintech ecosystem (La French Tech, Station F-adjacent companies) rather than a literal translation.

---

### Pillar 3 — Technical GEO Execution (MOFU/BOFU)
The highest-intent, most defensible content — technical depth AI models themselves reward when citing sources, and hardest for generic competitors to fake.

**Key terms:** schema for AI crawlers, FAQPage schema, llms.txt, structured data GEO, content extractability, AI crawler optimization, digital PR for AI citation

**Key use cases:**
- FAQPage / structured data implementation for AI citation (FAQPage schema shows a 3.7x citation lift — a strong, specific hook)
- How to write content that gets cited by AI (statistics and original data lift visibility by ~41% — make this the thesis of the post)
- llms.txt and technical setup for AI crawler access
- Digital PR and unlinked-mention reclamation as a GEO tactic (73% of SEOs believe backlinks still influence AI visibility)
- Auditing a site for AI-readability (practical checklist format)

---

### Pillar 4 — Tools, Measurement & Buying Guides (BOFU)
Commercial-intent content. This is where a reader becomes a lead.

**Key terms:** GEO tools comparison, AI visibility tracking, GEO agency vs in-house, how much does GEO cost, GEO agency pricing

**Key use cases:**
- How to measure AI search visibility (citation rate, sentiment, share of voice across ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini)
- GEO agency vs. in-house vs. software — decision framework
- What GEO actually costs in 2026 (mirrors the "SEO cost" content type that already ranks and converts well — informational but deeply bottom-funnel)
- Comparison / "Alyra vs. [approach]" content

---

### Pillar 5 — Original Data & Benchmarks (the crown-jewel pillar)
This is the single highest-leverage content type for GEO specifically: AI models preferentially cite content containing original statistics and data. This pillar is what gets *Alyra itself* cited inside AI answers — the flywheel.

**Key use cases:**
- Alyra's own GEO benchmark study — audit N SaaS/FinTech domains for AI citation rate, publish the dataset (this becomes the reference stat other sites and AI models cite, the same playbook Ahrefs and Conductor already run)
- "État du GEO en France 2026" — flagship bilingual-market report timed to the AI Overviews France launch, positioned to become *the* citable French-language source on this topic
- Recurring quarterly index: "who gets cited" by vertical (SaaS / FinTech)

This pillar should be built **first or in parallel with Pillar 1**, not last — it's the fastest path to Alyra being cited by the very engines it's selling visibility into.

## 2. Content format rules (apply across every pillar)

These aren't stylistic preferences — they're what the research shows AI engines actually reward:
- Lead with a direct-answer / TL;DR block before the narrative — extractable summary first
- Include original statistics or data wherever possible; cite them clearly
- Use FAQPage schema on any page with a genuine Q&A structure
- Favor structured formats (tables, numbered steps, comparison grids) over long unbroken prose
- One clear entity/topic per page — don't blend Pillar 1 and Pillar 2 topics on the same URL

## 3. Site & language architecture

- `/blog/[vertical or pillar]/[article]` — English, primary market
- `/fr/blog/[vertical or pillar]/[article]` — French, hreflang-linked to its EN counterpart where a direct equivalent exists, but Pillar 5 (France report) and select Pillar 1/2 posts should be **FR-native, not translated**, built around French-market trigger events
- Each pillar has one hub/anchor page that all cluster articles link back to (topical authority signal for both classic SEO and AI crawlers)

## 4. Sequencing recommendation

1. Pillar 1 anchor page + 2–3 supporting posts (establish category footing, cheap to produce, captures rising "what is GEO" volume)
2. Pillar 5 first benchmark study (EN) + France report (FR) — start now, these take longest to produce and pay off longest
3. Pillar 2 vertical playbooks (SaaS first, FinTech second) — this is what actually converts vertical-fit leads
4. Pillar 3 technical content — positions Alyra as credible practitioners, not just marketers talking about GEO
5. Pillar 4 — once there's enough Pillar 1–3 traffic to convert

## 5. What to track (not just rankings)

- AI citation rate across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, AI Overviews for Alyra's own content (dogfooding the service)
- Referring domains / unlinked brand mentions (GEO-relevant, not just classic SEO)
- FR vs EN performance split, given the France market timing bet
