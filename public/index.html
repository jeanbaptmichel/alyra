    FAQ

    Questions, answered plainly.

    What is AI visibility? +

    AI visibility is how often, and how favorably, your company shows up when people ask AI assistants questions related to your category.

    How is AI visibility different from SEO? +

    SEO optimizes for ranking in search results pages. AI visibility is about being part of the sources an AI assistant draws on to construct its answer, which isn't always the same set of pages that rank highest.

    Can Alyra guarantee that ChatGPT will mention my company? +

    No. AI answers are dynamic and no responsible provider can guarantee a specific recommendation or citation. Alyra focuses on identifying the sources that influence AI answers and increasing your presence within relevant, credible sources.

    Which AI assistants do you monitor? +

    We track prompts across the major AI assistants, including ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude, along with other AI search experiences as they gain adoption.

    Do you provide backlinks? +

    We may secure links where appropriate, but our objective is not backlink volume. We prioritize relevant third-party mentions and sources that matter within your category.

    How long does it take to see results? +

    It varies by category and how competitive it is. Monitoring starts immediately; source mapping and outreach typically build visibility over the following weeks and months.

    Do I need to change my website? +

    Not necessarily. Most of Alyra's work happens off your site, with third-party sources. We'll flag any on-site changes worth making if they'd meaningfully help.
